About The Movement

About New Nigeria Movement (NNM)

A citizen-powered political movement committed to reclaiming Nigeria's destiny through purposeful leadership, active participation, and community-driven development.

Our Vision

To build a prosperous, secure, united, and egalitarian Nigeria where every citizen has equal opportunity to thrive, innovate, and live in dignity, driven by world-class public service and patriotic leadership.

Our Mission

To mobilize, organize, and empower Nigerians across all strata of society to participate actively in democratic governance, install credible leaders, and enforce absolute accountability at all levels of government.

Policy Blueprint

The New Nigeria Manifesto

Our core development pillars to transform Nigeria into a beacon of African prosperity.

1. Economic Revitalization & Job Creation

Transitioning from a consumption-based economy to a high-value production and digital technology export hub. Supporting small and medium enterprises with low-interest funding and reliable power.

2. National Security & Rule of Law

Securing every inch of Nigerian territory through community policing, advanced intelligence technology, and strict adherence to constitutional justice without bias.

3. Universal Education & Healthcare Reform

Upgrading public schools to match global standards, providing free primary healthcare in all 8,812 wards, and eliminating medical tourism through world-class local facilities.
